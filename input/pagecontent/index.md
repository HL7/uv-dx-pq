<!-- WHY STRUCTURED PQ DATA -->
<div style="text-align:center; padding:48px 20px 16px;">
  <h2 style="font-size:1.5em; font-weight:800; color:#111827; margin-bottom:10px;">Why the industry is adopting structured PQ data</h2>
  <p style="font-size:.95em; color:#6b7280; max-width:540px; margin:0 auto 36px; line-height:1.55;">Move from unstructured PDFs and Word documents to a real-time, structured data model.</p>
</div>

<div style="display:grid; grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr; gap:16px; margin-bottom:48px;">
  <div style="background:#f9fafb; border:1px solid #e5e7eb; border-radius:10px; padding:24px;">
    <div style="font-size:1.4em; margin-bottom:10px;">⚡</div>
    <div style="font-weight:700; color:#003087; margin-bottom:6px;">Real-Time Data Exchange</div>
    <p style="font-size:.88em; color:#4b5563; line-height:1.6; margin:0;">Reduce pharmaceutical data exchange timelines from months to minutes using FHIR APIs. Enable high-frequency, high-volume data flows between sponsors, CMOs, and labs.</p>
  </div>
  <div style="background:#f9fafb; border:1px solid #e5e7eb; border-radius:10px; padding:24px;">
    <div style="font-size:1.4em; margin-bottom:10px;">♻️</div>
    <div style="font-weight:700; color:#003087; margin-bottom:6px;">Maximum Data Reuse</div>
    <p style="font-size:.88em; color:#4b5563; line-height:1.6; margin:0;">Granular, discrete components — like individual tests — can be centrally maintained, version-controlled, and reused across stability studies, specifications, and products.</p>
  </div>
  <div style="background:#f9fafb; border:1px solid #e5e7eb; border-radius:10px; padding:24px;">
    <div style="font-size:1.4em; margin-bottom:10px;">🤖</div>
    <div style="font-weight:700; color:#003087; margin-bottom:6px;">AI &amp; Data Science Ready</div>
    <p style="font-size:.88em; color:#4b5563; line-height:1.6; margin:0;">Highly granular structured data makes pharmaceutical quality information accessible for ontologies, data science, and artificial intelligence applications.</p>
  </div>
  <div style="background:#f9fafb; border:1px solid #e5e7eb; border-radius:10px; padding:24px;">
    <div style="font-size:1.4em; margin-bottom:10px;">🔗</div>
    <div style="font-weight:700; color:#003087; margin-bottom:6px;">Any Output Format</div>
    <p style="font-size:.88em; color:#4b5563; line-height:1.6; margin:0;">FHIR XML or JSON bundles can be transformed into any format required by regulators — PDF, Word, or jurisdiction-specific submission formats.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SCOPE -->
<h2 style="font-size:1.3em; font-weight:700; color:#111827; margin-bottom:12px;">Scope</h2>

<div style="display:grid; grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr; gap:14px; margin-bottom:32px;">
  <div style="background:#ecfdf5; border:1px solid #a7f3d0; border-radius:8px; padding:16px;">
    <div style="font-weight:700; color:#065f46; margin-bottom:6px; font-size:.9em;">✅ In Scope</div>
    <p style="font-size:.85em; color:#064e3b; line-height:1.6; margin:0;">Products regulated as drugs (small and large molecule medicinal products, including functional and non-functional packaging components; prescription, OTC, and hospital products). Developmental and authorized medicinal products. Data exchange within a biopharmaceutical company, between companies, or between companies and their industry stakeholders.</p>
  </div>
  <div style="background:#fef2f2; border:1px solid #fecaca; border-radius:8px; padding:16px;">
    <div style="font-weight:700; color:#991b1b; margin-bottom:6px; font-size:.9em;">🔜 Future Phases</div>
    <p style="font-size:.85em; color:#7f1d1d; line-height:1.6; margin:0;">Products regulated as devices and veterinary drugs are out of scope for version 1.0.0. New product lines will be brought in scope in future iterations of the implementation guide.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="background:#fffbeb; border:1px solid #fde68a; border-left:4px solid #d97706; border-radius:8px; padding:14px 18px; margin-bottom:32px; font-size:.85em; color:#78350f; display:flex; gap:10px; align-items:flex-start;">
  <span style="flex-shrink:0;">⚠️</span>
  <span><strong>Important:</strong> This implementation guide is a <strong>content exchange standard</strong> for industry use. It is <strong>not</strong> a regulatory submission standard and is not intended for direct submission to National Competent Authorities for medicinal product authorization. Sponsors should reference relevant national regulatory guidance when preparing submission data.</span>
</div>

<!-- STRATEGIC GOALS -->
<h2 style="font-size:1.3em; font-weight:700; color:#111827; margin-bottom:12px;">Strategic Goals</h2>

<p style="font-size:.92em; color:#374151; line-height:1.7; margin-bottom:16px;">The biopharmaceutical industry is transitioning into its third paradigm of data exchange. This IG accelerates that transition:</p>

<div style="display:grid; grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr 1fr; gap:14px; margin-bottom:24px;">
  <div style="background:#fff; border:1px solid #e5e7eb; border-top:3px solid #9ca3af; border-radius:8px; padding:18px 20px;">
    <div style="font-size:.72em; font-weight:700; text-transform:uppercase; letter-spacing:.1em; color:#9ca3af; margin-bottom:4px;">Era 1 · Pre-2003</div>
    <div style="font-weight:700; color:#111827; margin-bottom:6px;">Paper Documents</div>
    <div style="font-size:.82em; color:#6b7280; line-height:1.5;">Printed, published into binders, delivered to health authorities via courier. Manual, effort-intensive processes.</div>
  </div>
  <div style="background:#fff; border:1px solid #e5e7eb; border-top:3px solid #2563eb; border-radius:8px; padding:18px 20px;">
    <div style="font-size:.72em; font-weight:700; text-transform:uppercase; letter-spacing:.1em; color:#2563eb; margin-bottom:4px;">Era 2 · 2003–2023</div>
    <div style="font-weight:700; color:#111827; margin-bottom:6px;">Electronic Documents</div>
    <div style="font-size:.82em; color:#6b7280; line-height:1.5;">PDFs and Word files submitted via electronic gateways. Increased throughput but still limited by document-centric processes.</div>
  </div>
  <div style="background:#fff; border:1px solid #e5e7eb; border-top:3px solid #003087; border-radius:8px; padding:18px 20px;">
    <div style="font-size:.72em; font-weight:700; text-transform:uppercase; letter-spacing:.1em; color:#003087; margin-bottom:4px;">Era 3 · Post-2023</div>
    <div style="font-weight:700; color:#111827; margin-bottom:6px;">Structured Data &amp; APIs</div>
    <div style="font-size:.82em; color:#6b7280; line-height:1.5;">Granular FHIR data delivered via APIs, processed by AI agents. Advanced automation, high throughput.</div>
  </div>
</div>
