3.2.P.1 Description and Composition

Stelbatolol is formulated as an immediate-release, oral, solid dosage form in strengths of 20 mg of the free base equivalent. The 20 mg strength is presented as an orange, round, film coated tablet, debossed with '175' on one side.

Ingredient Role Amount per Tablet % w/w
Stelbatolol Active 15.2 mg 97.9%
Mannitol Excipient 145.0 mg 1.0%
Maize Starch Excipient 13.8 mg 0.5%
Calcium Carboxymethylcellulose Excipient 4.0 mg 0.25%
Magnesium Stearate Excipient 2.0 mg 0.35%
